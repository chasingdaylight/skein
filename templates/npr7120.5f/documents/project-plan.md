# Project plan

The agreement among the project manager, the program manager, the Center Director and the Mission Directorate Associate Administrator on how the project is carried out, after the template of NPR 7120.5F, Appendix H; each section is required, and one that does not apply says why.

## 1.0 Project overview

Introduction, objectives, mission description, and the project's organization and responsibilities.

## 2.0 Project baselines

Requirements, work breakdown structure, schedule (with the [key decision points](life-cycle.md)), resources and cost.

## 3.0 Project control plans

Technical, schedule and cost control; safety and mission assurance; risk management; acquisition; technology development; systems engineering; verification and validation; information technology; software; review; mission operations; environmental management; integrated logistics; science data management; configuration management; security; export control; knowledge management; human rating; planetary protection; and the others the template lists.

## 4.0 Waivers or deviations log

The tailoring of the [compliance matrix](pm-compliance.md), with the approval of each.
