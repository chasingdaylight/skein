# Compliance matrix rows

The rows of the compliance matrix of NPR 7120.7A (its Appendix C), each an item named by its table and its place in it (`ITA-C5.14` is the 14th row of Table C-5): the mandatory action, the product state it is due in (`Preliminary`, `Final` or `Update`), the requirement paragraphs it answers (`para`, as the table gives them), and the review it belongs to (`gate`). Where a paragraph names a requirement of [the requirements](requirements.md), the row links to it with `covers`. The columns the table leaves empty for the project, compliance (C, PC, NC, NA) and its justification, are the project's own: see the compliance rows of the project. Table C-1 holds the policy requirements of both service lines and projects; Tables C-2 and C-3 those of service lines, and Tables C-4 to C-9 those of projects.

Appendix D's Tables D-1 (service line gate products), D-2 (pre-formulation) and D-3 (project gate products) give the same product states as a grid of products by review, marked P for preliminary, F for final and U for updated; the `state` and `gate` of these rows are those cells. Its notes: "If Pre-Formulation includes TD refer to TD Gate products matrix - requirements listed for Pre-Formulation must still be addressed in the Compliance Matrix." and "Deployment is completed during the Deployment / O&M Phase and the results presented at PCR."
