# Systems engineering management plan

How the project carries out the systems engineering of NPR 7123.1D (chapter 6), approved by the Engineering Technical Authority (%[SE-06]) and kept current through the life cycle.

## Technical summary

The system, its context and boundaries, the product layers, and the documents that govern the work.

## The common technical processes

How the project performs the 17 common technical processes of chapter 3 and section 4.2, from stakeholder expectations definition to technical data management, and how they are tailored; the [compliance matrix](se-compliance.md) shows each requirement.

## Technical reviews

The reviews and their entrance and success criteria ([reviews](reviews.md)), peer reviews, and how their actions are tracked.

## Technical performance and risk

The technical performance measures and their margins (%[SE-60]), the technology readiness of critical technologies, and the technical risks.

## Integration, verification and validation

How the product is integrated, verified and validated, and the plans that say so.

## Organization and resources

The technical team, its responsibilities, and the people, facilities and tools it needs.
