# Results report

What a decision maker is told of results from a model (section 4.3.8 of NASA-STD-7009B; %[MS-32] to %[MS-39], %[MS-50]): one per analysis whose results inform a decision.

## The results and their uncertainty

The best estimate, its uncertainty, and how it was characterized.

## Credibility

The results assessment and the capability assessment of the model, factor by factor ([credibility](credibility.md)), against their thresholds.

## Use within limits

Whether the use was within the model's permissible uses and limits, and what follows if not.

## Deviations, risks and open issues

Deviations from the standard and their rationale, the risks of acting on the results, and open defects that bear on them.
