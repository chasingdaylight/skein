# Model and simulation plan

The plan for the acquisition, development, operation, maintenance and retirement of the models (%[MS-41]), with the organizations responsible, after section 4.1 of NASA-STD-7009B.

## Intended uses and criticality

For each model: its intended use and the decisions it informs (%[MS-40]), and its criticality (%[MS-06]); see [the register](models.md).

## Metrics and acceptance criteria

The programmatic and technical metrics kept (%[MS-42]), and the acceptance criteria for verification, validation, uncertainty and sensitivity, with the thresholds of the capability and results assessments (%[MS-43]).

## Development

The real-world system modeled and its data (%[MS-10]), the data sets and software used (%[MS-45]), units and coordinate frames (%[MS-46]), assumptions and abstractions (%[MS-11]), the concepts and mathematics (%[MS-12]), limits (%[MS-13]) and permissible uses (%[MS-14]); verification and validation, and their domains.

## Use

How a proposed use is checked against the permissible uses (%[MS-22]), and how results are assessed and reported.

## Configuration management, reviews and defects

How the models, their inputs and results are kept under configuration management; the technical reviews (%[MS-09]); defects tracked to closure (%[MS-51]).

## Recommended practices and training

The recommended practices adopted (section 4.1.3) and the training of developers, operators and analysts (section 4.1.4).
