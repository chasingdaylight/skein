# Risks of generative AI

The twelve risks of the generative AI profile of the AI RMF, each an item (`gen-ai.confabulation`) a project's AI system can be exposed to, with a short reading of what NIST means. For what to do about a risk, read the profile; its actions are organized under the subcategories of the [AI RMF core](core.md).

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.cbrn] **CBRN information or capabilities.** Easier access to information or design capabilities for chemical, biological, radiological or nuclear weapons, or other dangerous materials or agents.

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.confabulation] **Confabulation.** Confident but erroneous or false content, also called hallucination or fabrication, which can mislead the people who rely on it.

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.dangerous-content] **Dangerous, violent or hateful content.** Easier production of, and access to, violent, inciting, radicalizing or threatening content, including recommendations to harm oneself or others or to act illegally.

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.data-privacy] **Data privacy.** Leakage and unauthorized use, disclosure or de-anonymization of biometric, health, location or other personally identifiable or sensitive data.

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.environmental] **Environmental impacts.** The effects on ecosystems of the compute needed to train or operate the models.

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.harmful-bias] **Harmful bias and homogenization.** Amplified bias and societal disparities, and outputs that are less diverse than the world they describe, leading to discrimination or wrong assumptions about performance.

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.human-ai-configuration] **Human-AI configuration.** Poor arrangements or interactions between people and AI systems: over-reliance or automation bias, anthropomorphizing the system, or emotional entanglement with it.

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.information-integrity] **Information integrity.** A lower barrier to producing and spreading content that does not separate fact from opinion or fiction, or does not admit uncertainty, and that can drive large disinformation campaigns.

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.information-security] **Information security.** Lower barriers for offensive cyber capabilities, and a larger attack surface of the system itself: its training data, code and model weights.

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.intellectual-property] **Intellectual property.** Easier production or reproduction of copyrighted, trademarked or licensed content without authorization, and exposure of proprietary material.

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.obscene-content] **Obscene, degrading and abusive content.** Easier production of sexual, degrading or abusive content, including synthetic images of real people and child sexual abuse material.

%gen-ai-risk[gen-ai.value-chain] **Value chain and component integration.** Risks that enter through third-party components (data, models, services) that are hard to inspect, and through unclear responsibility along the supply chain.
