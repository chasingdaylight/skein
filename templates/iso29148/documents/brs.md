# Business requirements specification

What the organization needs and why (ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018, clause 9.3, in Skein's words). Its requirements are items of the type `business`, `BRS-001` and on.

## Business purpose and scope

Why the organization undertakes this, and what the business area covers.

## Business overview

The organization, its major stakeholders, and its environment: what constrains it from outside.

## Mission, goals and objectives

What the organization aims at, measurably.

## Business model and information environment

How it works, and the information it relies on.

## Business operational requirements

The processes, policies and rules it needs met.

%business[BRS-001 priority=high] The organization shall be able to see, for each product, which needs it meets. (An example: replace it.)

## Preliminary operational concept and scenarios

How the solution is expected to be used, and the scenarios that show it.
