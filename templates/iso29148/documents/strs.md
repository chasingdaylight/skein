# Stakeholder requirements specification

What the users and other stakeholders need of the system (ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018, clause 9.4, in skein's words). Its requirements are items of the type `stakeholder`, `STK-001` and on, each linked by `refines` to the business requirements it serves.

## Purpose, scope and overview

What the system is for, its boundaries, and the stakeholders.

## Business management requirements

What management of the business needs of it.

## Business operational requirements

What the business processes need of it.

## User requirements

What its users need to do with it, and how well.

%stakeholder[STK-001 user priority=high refines=BRS-001] A product manager needs to see the needs a product meets, from one place. (An example: replace it.)

## Operational concept and scenarios

How it will be used, the scenarios of use, and the conditions it will be used in.

## Project constraints

Cost, schedule and other limits on the project.
