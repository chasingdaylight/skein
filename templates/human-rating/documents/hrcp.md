# Human-Rating Certification Package

The plans, documents and summaries that NPR 8705.2C asks of a crewed space system for its Human-Rating Certification (%[HRC-2.2.1]; its Appendix D), and when: I is the first release, U an update, X a one-time item, at the System Requirements, System Definition, Preliminary Design, Critical Design and Operational Readiness Reviews. Each section answers the requirements it names; replace the prompts with the program's text, or with references to the program documents that hold it.

## What is due at each review

```skein
list hrc columns=title,srr,sdr,pdr,cdr,orr
```

## Process and standards

Waivers, deviations and exceptions (%[HRC-2.2.2]); the safety analysis process (%[HRC-2.2.3]); the safety and mission assurance plan (%[HRC-2.2.4]); the standards the Technical Authorities mandate (%[HRC-2.2.6]) and their tailoring (%[HRC-2.2.7]).

## Designing the system

The system and its reference missions (%[HRC-2.3.1]); the crew survival strategy (%[HRC-2.3.2]); the design philosophy for using the crew (%[HRC-2.3.3]); how the survival capabilities (%[HRC-2.3.4]) and the technical requirements of NASA-STD-8719.29 (%[HRC-2.3.5]) are implemented; the probabilistic safety requirements (%[HRC-2.3.6]); risks and uncertainties (%[HRC-2.3.7.2]); crew workload (%[HRC-2.3.9]); human-in-the-loop evaluation (%[HRC-2.3.10.2], %[HRC-2.3.10.3]); human error analysis (%[HRC-2.3.11.2]).

## Verifying and validating the system

How the technical requirements (%[HRC-2.4.1]), survival capabilities (%[HRC-2.4.2]), critical systems (%[HRC-2.4.3], %[HRC-2.4.4]) and critical software (%[HRC-2.4.5.1], %[HRC-2.4.5.2]) are verified and validated, and the results (%[HRC-2.4.6], %[HRC-2.4.7], %[HRC-2.4.8]); crew workload validated (%[HRC-2.4.9]); the safety models updated (%[HRC-2.4.10]).

## Flight testing the system

The flight test program (%[HRC-2.5.1], %[HRC-2.5.2]) and its results (%[HRC-2.5.3]).

## Certifying and operating the system

Configuration management and maintenance (%[HRC-2.6.1]); data collection, management and analysis (%[HRC-2.6.2]).
