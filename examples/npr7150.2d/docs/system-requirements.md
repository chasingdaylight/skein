# Rover system requirements

The rover's system requirements that the drive software's requirements come from. They belong to the rover's systems engineering, and are here because this example has no other repository; a real project keeps them where the system's requirements are and refers to them across repositories.

%system[SYS-001] The rover shall come to rest when it loses its link to the ground station.

%system[SYS-002] The rover shall drive at walking pace or slower in the test field.

%system[SYS-003] The rover shall keep a record of its commands for the test's analysis.
